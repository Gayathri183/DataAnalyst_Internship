# DataAnalyst_Internship
# Competitor Analysis of Top 100 Indian Recruitment Agencies

#Project Overview

This project was part of my internship with **Analytics Career Connect**, where our client (confidential) was planning to launch a new HR & Recruitment Agency in India.
The objective was to conduct a **competitor analysis of the Top 100 recruitment agencies** in India to identify:

* Market trends
* Service gaps
* Opportunities for differentiation

The final deliverables included a **clean dataset, Power BI dashboard, and a competitor analysis report**.



#Objectives

* Collect structured data of 100 recruitment agencies.
* Analyze competitors by **services, industries, geography, and digital presence**.
* Identify top players, emerging players, and underserved markets.
* Deliver insights through **interactive dashboards** and a **summary report**.


#Data Collected

For each agency, the dataset includes:

* Agency Name & Website
* Headquarters / Locations
* Founded Year
* Industries Served
* Services Offered (Permanent Staffing, RPO, Executive Search, etc.)
* Clients / Partners (if available)
* Social Media Presence (LinkedIn, Glassdoor, etc.)
* Company Size / Revenue (if available)
* Unique Selling Proposition (USP)
* Geographical Coverage (Tier-1, Tier-2/3, International)
* Technology Adoption (AI, ATS, HR Tech)
* Awards & Certifications



 #Business Questions Answered

* Which cities have the highest concentration of recruitment agencies?
* What are the top industries served by competitors?
* Which services are most commonly offered?
* How many agencies provide IT vs. non-IT hiring?
* Which agencies have the strongest digital presence?
* Are Tier-2 & Tier-3 cities underserved?
* What are the unique USPs of top players?
* Where are the market gaps & opportunities for entry?



 #Tools & Technologies

* **Python**: BeautifulSoup, Selenium, Scrapy (Web Scraping)
* **Pandas, Excel**: Data Cleaning & Structuring
* **Power BI**: Visualization & Insights
* **MS Word / Google Docs**: Reporting


#Deliverables

* **Competitor Dataset (Excel/CSV)** – Clean dataset with 100 agencies.
* **Power BI Dashboard** – Includes:

  * Agency Distribution by City (Map & Bar Chart)
  * Industry Coverage (Treemap / Bar Chart)
  * Services Offered (Stacked Bar Chart)
  * Digital Presence Scorecard
  * Top Agencies by Scale
* **Competitor Analysis Report** (2–3 pages) with:

  * Key Findings
  * Market Gaps
  * Recommendations for client entry strategy



#Project Timeline (4 Days)

1. Day 1 – Competitor identification & Data Collection
2. Day 2 – Data Cleaning & Structuring
3. Day 3 – Data Analysis & Insight Generation
4. Day 4 – Dashboard Development & Final Report Submission



#Outcome

By the end of this project, the client had a **comprehensive competitor analysis dashboard** and report to support their market entry strategy into the recruitment industry.
